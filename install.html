ZeeMo

Put ZeeMo on your phone

Private messaging for schools, offices and cooperatives. Free, and it installs in under a minute, with no app store needed.

Scan or open the link

Point your phone camera at the code, or type this address in your browser:

zeemoapp.github.io
Open ZeeMo

ZeeMo is installed on this device. Look for it on your home screen.

How to install

Your device

Android

Use Chrome

  1. Open zeemoapp.github.io in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when it appears, or tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. ZeeMo appears on your home screen.
Your device

iPhone

Use Safari

  1. Open zeemoapp.github.io in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Your device

Computer

Use Chrome or Edge

  1. Open zeemoapp.github.io.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or the ⋮ menu, then Install ZeeMo.
  3. ZeeMo opens in its own window.

Good to know

  • Sign in with your phone number or email. You get a 6-digit code, then you set your own passcode.
  • The app locks itself when you leave it. Your chats are never stored on the phone.
  • Updates arrive by themselves. Close the app fully and open it again to get the latest version.
  • Message alerts (notifications) are coming soon, together with the Google Play version.
